AI Hype: What's Real, What's Missing, and What It Costs is a short cartoon starring Thor and Donovan, by D.A. Williams. This page lists every factual claim in the cartoon, line by line, with the primary source behind it.

Sources were checked on November 21, 2026 (America/Phoenix). Prices, limits, and terms change, so open the official page yourself before you rely on an offer. The social posts are teaching examples, not a survey of how common hype is. No software in the cartoon was installed, tested, or security-audited.

T12, on-screen posterA headline said "Agents outwork employees six to one" and "junior hiring has collapsed."Sourced (example)The headline read: "NEW: Anthropic's AI Agents Now Outwork Employees 6-to-1 - and Junior Hiring Has Collapsed."Example post: @nickthorpp on X (6-to-1 headline)
T13The ratio counted employee-directed agent runtime against a 40-hour human workweek.SourcedAnthropic: agent runtime includes "inference and active tool use, but excluding any wait longer than 30 minutes. Human work hours are calculated as headcount and assumed 40 hours per week."Anthropic, "Employment at Anthropic" (research, Oct 16, 2026)
T14Not six times the value, skill, or completed jobs.Sourced (reading of metric)The 6:1 figure is a ratio of runtime hours to assumed human hours. The report does not present it as value, skill, or completed work.Anthropic, "Employment at Anthropic" (research, Oct 16, 2026)
D13A smaller share of junior hires isn't a hiring collapse when the company is growing.SourcedAnthropic: the junior share of technical employees fell from 21% to 14%, and the junior share of technical hires fell from 27% to 9%. The report also says: "As the company expands, technical hiring has continued growing in absolute terms."Anthropic, "Employment at Anthropic" (research, Oct 16, 2026)
T15The authors warned adoption wasn't random, and growth complicates cause and effect.SourcedAnthropic: "tool adoption is not random ... this would bias our estimates."Anthropic, "Employment at Anthropic" (research, Oct 16, 2026)
D14A preview isn't a general release.General guidance (example)An example of a preview-build claim. Whether it has shipped publicly, and to which accounts, was not established. This is a caution, not a claim about any product.Example post: @orendev_ on X (preview-build example)
T16Local files don't mean every model call stays on your machine.SourcedOpen WebUI connects the same local interface to "cloud APIs." OpenMausBot is "local-first, on the models you" choose. Picking a cloud model sends your prompts to that provider.Open WebUI docs, "Connect a Provider"
OpenMausBot repository README (GitHub)
T17Turning history off isn't the same as gone; one official privacy page says those chats can still be kept for about two months; training can be a separate switch.Sourced (quote)Google: "Even if Gemini History is turned off, future chats will still be saved for around 2 months." The blog post describes new settings that let users opt out of model training separately from history.Google, Gemini Apps Privacy Hub
Google, "Manage & delete your history in Gemini Apps"

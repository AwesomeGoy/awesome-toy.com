Christmas around the world coloring book for kids

Christmas Around the World: A Coloring Trip Through 8 Cities

by D.A. Williams

A children's coloring book by D.A. Williams. Visit 8 cities and discover how families celebrate Christmas: the giant tree at Rockefeller Center in New York, the tree Norway sends to London every year, the old Christmas markets of Strasbourg and Nuremberg, the nativity scene in Rome, Las Posadas in Mexico City, fried chicken and strawberry cake in Tokyo, and a sunny beach Christmas in Sydney. About this book

This 38-page coloring book is by D.A. Williams. It has 8 large coloring pages with bold, clean lines, a true, kid-friendly fact page and a "My Postcard" drawing page for every city, and a world map to find each stop and color. Single-sided coloring pages, so markers won't ruin the next picture. Large 8.5 x 11 inch pages, for kids ages 4-10. Paperback, $9.99.

D.A. Williams lives in Arizona. This coloring book is published by Red Pills of Freedom and sold on Amazon.

What is inside

This Arizona desert coloring book is for relaxing, stress-relief coloring. The designs are inspired by the plants and blooms of the Arizona desert, with detailed swirling borders around each one. One design is printed per page, on one side.

The sample pages show the range. One is a desert plant in an oval frame. One is a cactus bloom beside a spiny stem, so cactus appears here too, inside the fractal borders rather than as a mandala. One is a palm surrounded by the same style of swirling line. The free printable is a further page: fruit and leaves inside a swirling border, as an 8.5x11 PDF for personal use.

How it differs from the mandala book

Arizona Desert Bloom Mandalas is the companion adult coloring book, and it is built differently. Every mandala began as a photograph of a real plant growing in Arizona. Texas sage, saguaro, prickly pear, cholla, yellow bells, barrel cactus, and two dozen more were each turned into one of 30 mandalas, with a full-color reference and a line-art page. This fractal book does not use that photo reference or those named-plant mandalas.

D.A. Williams lives in Arizona and writes both coloring books, plus children's stories about Max and the robot dog Thor. Arizona Desert Bloom Fractals is published by Red Pills of Freedom and sold on Amazon.

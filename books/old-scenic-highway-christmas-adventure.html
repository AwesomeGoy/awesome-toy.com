The Winslow Christmas trip

Max and Thor head to Winslow, Arizona, for a Christmas trip on an old scenic highway. Thor is a posh know-it-all robot dog. He finds his way into the RV's computer and talks his way into the driver's seat, which leads to a hailstorm mix-up at the car wash. They visit a historic railroad hotel, spot petroglyphs and racing pronghorn in a state park, and look over the rim of a giant meteor crater. On Christmas morning, Thor's secret project is revealed: a handmade toy train with a bone in the caboose.

The cover calls this a Robot Dog Series Christmas special. Sample pages from this full-color edition show Donovan with Max and Thor. Their first stop in the samples is Standin' on the Corner Park in Winslow. Another page shows a flatbed truck carrying a Christmas tree. A third shows pronghorn racing across grassland while a hawk circles.

Full color, not the coloring book

Choose this edition when you want the finished pictures. The coloring-book edition tells this same Winslow trip with black-and-white line art on every story page, so kids can color the adventure as they read. A free 8.5x11 page from that coloring edition, the car-wash hailstorm, is on the free printables page. This full-color book does not have a printable coloring page.

D.A. Williams lives in Arizona and writes children's stories about Max and Thor on adventures around the state. This story is published by Red Pills of Freedom and sold on Amazon.

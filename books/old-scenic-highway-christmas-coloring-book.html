The story you color

Max and Thor head to Winslow, Arizona, for a Christmas trip on an old scenic highway. Thor is a posh know-it-all robot dog. He finds his way into the RV's computer and talks his way into the driver's seat, which leads to a hailstorm mix-up at the car wash. They visit a historic railroad hotel, spot petroglyphs and racing pronghorn in a state park, and look over the rim of a giant meteor crater. On Christmas morning, Thor's secret project is revealed: a handmade toy train with a bone in the caboose.

The cover calls this a Robot Dog Series Christmas special, the same special as the full-color book, drawn in line art instead of finished color. The free sample page shows the car-wash mix-up: Thor convinces Donovan he is fit to drive, and hail sends them looking for shelter. Look-inside pages show line art of Standin' on the Corner Park in Winslow, a freight train rolling through town, and pronghorn on the grassland with Donovan, Max, and Thor.

Coloring book, not the full-color story

Choose this edition to color. The full-color Christmas book for kids tells the same Winslow trip with the pictures already colored. Read that one when you want the painted pages. Color this one when you want the line art.

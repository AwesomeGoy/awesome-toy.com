Max, Thor and the Old Scenic Highway Christmas Adventure: A Robot Dog Christmas Coloring Book Story

by D.A. Williams

Max and Thor head to Winslow, Arizona, for a Christmas trip on an old scenic highway. Thor, the posh know-it-all robot dog, finds his way into the RV's computer and talks his way into the driver's seat, which leads to a hailstorm mix-up at the car wash. They visit a historic railroad hotel, spot petroglyphs and racing pronghorn in a state park, and look over the rim of a giant meteor crater. On Christmas morning, Thor's secret project is revealed: a handmade toy train with a bone in the caboose.

This edition has black-and-white line art on every story page, so kids can color the adventure as they read.

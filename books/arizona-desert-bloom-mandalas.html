What is inside

Every design in this book began as a photograph of a real plant growing in Arizona. Texas sage, saguaro, prickly pear, cholla, yellow bells, barrel cactus, and two dozen more were each turned into an original, detailed mandala. The cover calls it a full-color guide for every design. In the book, the 30 mandalas run from simple to intricate, and each one is paired with a line-art page for relaxing, stress-relief coloring.

Saguaro, prickly pear, cholla, and barrel cactus make this a cactus coloring book as well as an Arizona desert coloring book. The other designs come from desert shrubs and blooms, including Texas sage and yellow bells, so the pages are not limited to cactus.

The Saguaro Sunset sample shows how a design is presented. The colored mandala sits with the original photograph, and the reference names the plant Carnegiea gigantea. The next sample is that same mandala as line art. A third sample is a different line-art mandala, this one built from snow-dusted saguaros.

How it differs from the fractal book

Arizona Desert Bloom Fractals is the other adult coloring book from D.A. Williams. It uses fractal patterns and detailed swirling borders inspired by the plants and blooms of the Arizona desert, with one design per page, printed on one side. It does not turn named plants into the 30 mandalas, and it does not include the full-color photo reference this book gives each design.

D.A. Williams lives in Arizona. Arizona Desert Bloom Mandalas is published by Red Pills of Freedom and sold on Amazon. A free round desert-flower mandala from the book is on the printables page as an 8.5x11 PDF for personal use.

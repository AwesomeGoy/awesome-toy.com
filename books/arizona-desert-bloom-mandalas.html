Arizona Desert Bloom Mandalas: Adult Coloring Book: 30 Mandalas Inspired by Real Arizona Desert Plants

by D.A. Williams

Every design in this book began as a photograph of a real plant growing in Arizona. Texas sage, saguaro, prickly pear, cholla, yellow bells, barrel cactus and two dozen more were each turned into an original, detailed mandala.

30 mandalas from simple to intricate, each with a full-color reference and a line-art page to color. A relaxing, stress-relief coloring book for adults.

About this book

This 28-page coloring book is by D.A. Williams. It has twenty original desert critters, one large coloring page each, with bold, clean lines and a short line on every page naming the real animal and plant behind each critter. Large 8.5 x 11 inch pages, for kids ages 4-10. Paperback, $9.99.

D.A. Williams lives in Arizona. This coloring book is published by Red Pills of Freedom and sold on Amazon.

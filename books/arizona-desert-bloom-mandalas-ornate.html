Arizona Desert Bloom Mandalas

Ornate Edition: Adult Coloring Book with 30 Intricate Mandalas Inspired by Real Arizona Desert Plants

by D.A. Williams

The companion to Arizona Desert Bloom Mandalas. Thirty real Arizona desert plants become richly detailed mandalas, each framed by an ornate border, with a full-color guide facing every coloring page.

What is inside

Every design began as a photograph of a plant growing in Arizona, including Texas sage, saguaro, prickly pear, cholla, hedgehog cactus, lupine, and more. This Ornate Edition is for colorists who like finer work: each of the 30 mandalas has its own ornate border, such as pearls, lotus petals, rope, lace, scrolls, or plant-motif bands.

A finished full-color version faces every coloring page, as a guide. The plant's name and a small thumbnail of the original photo appear with each color guide. Coloring pages are printed on the right-hand page, on 8.5x11 in paper. The book also includes a "test your colors" page and an "About the Plants" page.

How it differs from the first mandala book

Arizona Desert Bloom Mandalas is the companion volume. It also turns photographs of real Arizona desert plants into 30 mandalas, each with a full-color reference and a line-art page. This Ornate Edition uses more intricate designs and gives every mandala its own decorative border.

D.A. Williams lives in Arizona. Arizona Desert Bloom Mandalas: Ornate Edition is published by Red Pills of Freedom and sold on Amazon.

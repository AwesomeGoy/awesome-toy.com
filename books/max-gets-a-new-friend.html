The story you color

Bella is back! Max's returning friend has come to visit, and the two besties pick up right where they left off: zoomies, tug-of-war, snack time, blue bush ambushes, belly-up naps and the best car wash ride ever.

Thor, the very proper robot dog, has rules. Nobody listens. He also explains oxymorons, like "quick nap" and "Max is sharing." This is a Robot Dog Series coloring book story for kids 4 to 10 and the grown-ups reading along.

D.A. Williams lives in Arizona. This coloring book is published by Red Pills of Freedom and sold on Amazon.

About D.A. Williams, Arizona author

D.A. Williams lives in Arizona and writes children's stories and coloring books for adults. Awesome Goy is the humorous home of these books on the web. They are published by Red Pills of Freedom and sold on Amazon.

Children's stories

The children's stories star two dogs, Max and Thor, on adventures around Arizona. Thor is a posh know-it-all robot dog. Their Christmas book for kids follows a holiday trip to Winslow, Arizona, on an old scenic highway. The cover calls it a Robot Dog Series Christmas special.

That Christmas story is published in two editions. The full-color book is a picture story with the art already in color. The coloring book uses black-and-white line art on every story page so kids can color the adventure as they read. Sample pages show Donovan traveling with Max and Thor, including a stop at Standin' on the Corner Park in Winslow.

Adult coloring books

The coloring books for adults take their designs from real Arizona desert plants. Arizona Desert Bloom Mandalas turns photographs of those plants into 30 mandalas. Texas sage, saguaro, prickly pear, cholla, yellow bells, barrel cactus, and two dozen more each became an original mandala, with a full-color reference and a line-art page. It is a cactus coloring book and an Arizona desert coloring book in one volume.

Arizona Desert Bloom Fractals is the other adult coloring book. Its pages are fractal designs inspired by desert plants and blooms, with detailed swirling borders. One design is printed per page, on one side, for relaxing, stress-relief coloring. It does not use the 30 named-plant mandalas or the full-color photo reference from the mandala book.

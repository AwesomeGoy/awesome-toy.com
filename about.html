D.A. Williams

D.A. Williams lives in Arizona and writes children's stories and coloring books.

The children's stories star two dogs, Max and Thor, on adventures around Arizona. The coloring books for adults take their designs from real Arizona desert plants.

Awesome Goy

Awesome Goy is the humorous home of these books on the web. They are published by Red Pills of Freedom and sold on Amazon.
